## ❓ Product Hunt Launch FAQs

**Q: Do you save or store my uploaded Excel/CSV files?**  
**A:** No. Spreadsheet analysis runs in your browser, and tools that need our server process the file in memory and discard it. We never keep your files, their names or their contents.

**Q: How does this differ from standard AI presentation builders?**  
**A:** Standard tools require manual prompt engineering or store your raw documents in persistent vector databases. Meldra is purpose-built for structured spreadsheet analysis, generating native `.pptx` and interactive charts and never stores your files.

**Q: Can I use my company's branding?**  
**A:** Yes. Set your brand colour, font, company name and logo, and every generated deck uses them. (Uploading your own `.pptx` template is on the roadmap.)
