## ❓ Product Hunt Launch FAQs

**Q: Do you save or store my uploaded Excel/CSV files?**  
**A:** Never. All processing happens in-memory (RAM) and files are completely discarded immediately after output creation.

**Q: How does this differ from standard AI presentation builders?**  
**A:** Standard tools require manual prompt engineering or store your raw documents in persistent vector databases. InsightSheet is purpose-built for structured spreadsheet analysis, generating native `.pptx` and interactive charts with strict enterprise privacy guarantees.

**Q: Can enterprise teams customize corporate slide templates?**  
**A:** Yes! Premium and Team licenses allow uploading custom corporate PowerPoint templates (`.pptx`) so generated slides automatically inherit your company colors, fonts, and branding.
