The **Settings** page holds your profile, devices, language, branding and theme, password reset and account deletion. This article explains each section.

## Open Settings

1. Open your account menu at the top right of the page (it shows your email address).
2. Select **Settings**.

## Profile

**Profile** shows the **Email** you sign in with and your **Full name**. You cannot change your email address here.

> **Note:** Changing your name is not available yet. If you select **Save Profile**, the page shows "Profile update is coming soon."

## Signed-in devices

**Signed-in devices** lists every device where your account is signed in. Your account can be signed in on two devices at the same time.

Each device shows its browser and device type, an approximate location, its IP address and when it was last active. Your current device is marked "(this device)".

To sign a device out:

1. Find the device in the list.
2. Select **Sign out**.

That device must sign in again, with a new login code, before it can be used. You cannot sign out the device you are using from this list. Use **Logout** in your account menu instead.

## Language

1. Under **Language**, open **App language**.
2. Choose **English (Default)**, **Deutsch (German)** or **Français (French)**.
3. Select **Save Language**.

The language is saved in this browser.

> **Note:** Some screens are not translated yet and still appear in English.

## Branding and theme

Under **Branding & Theme** you can show your own name and logo in the meldra header, and change the colours.

1. Enter a **Brand name**.
2. Add a logo. Either paste a link in **Logo URL (optional)**, or select **Upload logo** and choose a PNG, JPG, SVG or WebP image up to 300 KB.
3. Choose a **Primary color** with the colour picker, or type a hex code such as `#0ea5e9`.
4. Choose a **Theme**: **System**, **Light** or **Dark**.
5. Select **Save Branding**.

Select **Reset** to go back to the default branding and the **System** theme.

> **Note:** Branding and theme settings are saved in this browser only. An uploaded logo is not sent to meldra's servers. Set them again on each browser you use.

## Change your password

1. Under **Change Password**, check the **Email**.
2. Select **Send Reset Email**.
3. Open the email and follow the link to choose a new password.

For more detail, see [Sign-in and security](/help/sign-in-and-security).

## Delete your account

Deleting your account permanently removes your account, sign-in and device history, usage history, API keys and any data you stored in meldra. Payment records are kept for as long as tax law requires, without your IP address or browser details.

1. Under **Delete my account**, enter **Your password**.
2. In **Type DELETE to confirm**, type `DELETE`.
3. Select **Delete my account permanently**.

The account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

> **Important:** Deleting your account cannot be undone.

## Export your data

The **Settings** page does not have a data export. For questions about the data meldra holds about you, email support@meldra.ai.
