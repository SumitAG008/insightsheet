An organisation licence gives your people seats on the Team or Business plan for a fixed term. This article is for organisation owners and admins. It explains the **Organisation** page and how seats, members and renewals work.

## What a licence includes

- A number of seats for a fixed term, on the Team or Business plan.
- An admin page to add and remove people, set roles, see usage per person, export it as CSV and see the change history.
- Optional sign-up by email domain.
- Invoice and purchase-order billing.
- Custom limits where agreed.
- A data processing agreement.
- A grace period after the end date (14 days by default) while the renewal is processed.

To buy or change a licence, contact Meldra. See [Plans and limits](/help/plans-and-limits).

## Open the Organisation page

1. Open your account menu at the top right and select **Plan and usage**.
2. Select **Manage** and your organisation's name. This button appears for owners and admins.

You can also go straight to [insight.meldra.ai/organization](https://insight.meldra.ai/organization).

Members who are not admins see the licence details and the note "Your organisation's admins manage seats and members."

## Check your licence

The **Licence** card shows:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Plan** | The plan your members get. |
| **Seats used** | Seats taken out of seats bought. Shown in amber when all are in use. |
| **Renews** | The licence end date. |
| **Status** | The licence state: active, pending (not started yet), grace, expired, suspended or cancelled. |

## Add a person

1. Under **Add a person**, enter their email address.
2. Choose a role: **Member** or **Admin**. Owners can also choose **Owner**.
3. Select **Add**.

Meldra confirms that the person has a seat. They sign in to Meldra with that email to use it. Until they create an account, the members list shows "(not signed up yet)" next to their email.

> **Note:** When all seats are in use, **Add** is unavailable. Remove someone, or contact sales@meldra.ai to add seats.

## Change a role

In **Members and usage this month**, choose a new role in the **Role** column. Only an owner can make someone an owner or change an owner's role.

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Member** | Use Meldra with the licence's limits. |
| **Admin** | Everything a member can, plus manage seats and members. |
| **Owner** | Everything an admin can, plus give or change the owner role. |

## Remove a person

1. In **Members and usage this month**, find the person.
2. Select the bin icon at the end of their row.
3. Confirm. Their seat becomes free and they move to their own plan.

Nothing in their account is deleted.

## See usage per person

**Members and usage this month** shows, for each person, **Conversions**, **AI questions**, **Uploaded (MB)** and **Last seen**.

Select **Export CSV** to download the figures as `meldra-usage.csv`.

> **Note:** Usage is counts only. Meldra never stores or shows file names or file contents.

## See the change history

**Change history (90 days)** lists each change to your organisation: when it happened, who made it and what changed.

## Sign-up by email domain

Your licence can include domain sign-up. Anyone who signs in with your organisation's email domain then gets a seat automatically while seats remain. Public email domains, such as gmail.com, cannot be used. Ask Meldra to switch it on or off when you set up or change your licence.

## How limits work for members

- While the licence is active, or in its grace period, members get the licence's plan limits and any custom limits on it.
- If a member's own paid plan is higher for a limit, they keep their own figure.

## Renewal and the grace period

- After the end date, the licence enters a grace period, 14 days by default. Members keep access, and **Plan and usage** tells them the licence ended.
- On renewal, members keep their seats.
- If the licence is not renewed, or is suspended or cancelled, members move to their own plan's limits automatically. Nothing is deleted.
