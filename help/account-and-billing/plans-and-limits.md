This article lists the plans, their prices and limits, and explains how to check your usage and what to do when you reach a limit.

## Prices

UK prices, before VAT:

| Plan | Price |
|---|---|
| **Free** | £0 |
| **Pro** (one person) | £25 a month, or £250 a year |
| **Team** (from 3 users) | £15 per user a month, or £150 per user a year |
| **Business and universities** | Custom annual licence |

Paying yearly gives two months free. VAT at 20% is added where it applies. Prices are shown in your local currency (GBP, EUR, USD or INR) based on your location.

## Limits

Limits apply per user.

| Limit | Free | Pro | Team | Business |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Largest single file | 10 MB | 100 MB | 100 MB | 200 MB, or as agreed |
| Rows per spreadsheet (all sheets together) | 50,000 | 300,000 | 1,000,000 | 2,000,000, or as agreed |
| Pages per PDF | 50 | 500 | 1,000 | 2,000, or as agreed |
| Scanned pages read by OCR per file | 5 | 100 | 100 | 300, or as agreed |
| Conversions and file jobs per month | 20 | 1,000 | 2,000 | 5,000, or as agreed |
| AI questions per month | 20 | 500 | 1,000 | 3,000, or as agreed |
| Total uploads per month | 200 MB | 10 GB | 20 GB | 50 GB, or as agreed |
| Files processing at the same time | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |

Business and university licences can have custom limits agreed in the contract.

Monthly allowances reset on the 1st of each month.

> **Note:** Unified Reporting reads uploaded files in your browser, up to 50 MB per file. See [Add your data](/help/add-your-data).

## See your usage

1. Open your account menu at the top right of the page (it shows your email address).
2. Select **Plan and usage**.

The page shows:

- your plan, and the organisation it comes from if you have a seat on a licence;
- **Conversions and file jobs this month**, **AI questions this month** and **Uploads this month**, each with a meter and its reset date;
- your **Per file** limits;
- **All plans**, a table comparing every plan.

Select **Refresh** to update the figures. Meters turn amber at 70% and red at 90%.

## When you reach a limit

| Limit reached | What you see |
|---|---|
| File too large | "File size (… MB) exceeds … MB limit on the … plan." |
| Too many rows | "This spreadsheet has more than … rows, the most the … plan allows per file. Split it or upgrade your plan." |
| Too many PDF pages | "This PDF has … pages; the … plan allows … per file. Split it into smaller PDFs or upgrade your plan." |
| Too many OCR pages | Only the first pages up to your limit are read. |
| Monthly conversions used | "You have used all … conversions for this month. They reset on the 1st, or upgrade for more." |
| Monthly AI questions used | "You have used all … AI questions for this month. They reset on the 1st, or upgrade for more." |
| Monthly uploads used | "Monthly upload limit exceeded (… MB/month)." |

> **Tip:** When meldra is very busy, a file may wait a few seconds for its turn. The page retries by itself.

## Upgrade your plan

1. On **Plan and usage**, select **Compare plans**.
2. On the pricing page, fill in **Request pricing** and select **Send pricing request**, or email pricing@meldra.ai.

We reply by email with pricing for your needs.
