The **Plan and usage** page shows your plan, how much of this month's allowance you have used and the limits that apply to each file. This article explains what each part of the page means.

For the figures on each plan, see [Plans and limits](/help/plans-and-limits).

## Open the page

1. Open your account menu at the top right of the page (it shows your email address).
2. Select **Plan and usage**.

Select **Refresh** at any time to load the latest figures.

## Your plan

The heading shows your plan, for example **Pro plan**. If your plan comes from an organisation licence, the heading adds "through" and your organisation's name.

If your organisation's licence has ended, a message tells you:

- During the grace period: you keep access while the licence is renewed.
- After the grace period: your own plan's limits apply. Ask your organisation's admin about renewal.

See [Organisations and team licences](/help/organisations-and-team-licences).

## Monthly meters

Each meter shows what you have used and your limit, for example "120 / 1,000". It shows **Unlimited** when there is no limit. Under each meter, **Resets** gives the date the count goes back to zero.

| Meter | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Conversions and file jobs this month** | One for each conversion or file job you run, such as converting a document or processing a file. Each run of the [Web Data Connector](/help/collect-data-from-websites) also counts as one. |
| **AI questions this month** | One for each request to meldra's AI, however long the question or answer. |
| **Uploads this month** | The total size, in MB, of the files you send to meldra's servers for processing. |

The bar is green while you have plenty left. It turns amber at 70% of the limit and red at 90%.

> **Note:** When a meter is full, meldra stops that kind of work until the reset date. See [Plans and limits](/help/plans-and-limits) for the messages you see and how to upgrade.

## Per file

**Per file** lists the limits that apply to each file you process:

| Limit | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Largest single file (MB)** | The biggest file you can upload. |
| **Rows per spreadsheet (all sheets together)** | The most rows in one workbook, counting every sheet. |
| **Pages per PDF** | The most pages in one PDF. |
| **Scanned pages read by OCR per file** | How many scanned pages OCR reads in one file. Later pages are not read. |
| **Files processing at the same time** | How many of your files can be processed at once. |

When meldra is very busy, a file may wait a few seconds for its turn. The page retries by itself.

## Buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Refresh** | Reloads your plan and usage. |
| **Compare plans** | Opens the pricing page. |
| **Manage** and your organisation's name | Opens the **Organisation** page. Shown to organisation owners and admins only. |

## All plans

**All plans** is a table comparing the limits of every plan. Your current plan is highlighted and marked "Your plan".
