meldra Insight turns spreadsheets, PDFs and system exports into answers, reports and load-ready files. This article explains what each tool does, how to sign up and how to find your way around.

## What you can do

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Unified Reporting** | Combine exports from several systems (for example HR, finance and sales) and ask questions across them in plain English. You get a chart, a written answer and the sources behind every number. |
| **Migration** | Turn an HR extract from your current system into SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central import files, in load order, with a review workbook. |
| Ask your data | Ask a question about a spreadsheet in plain English and get the answer, a chart or a summary. |
| P&L from a sentence | Describe the profit and loss statement you need and get a formatted P&L workbook. |
| Excel to PowerPoint | Turn a workbook into board-ready slides with charts. |
| Reconciliation | Match two files (bank, ledger, supplier statement) and see what does not agree. |
| Invoice and statement extraction | Pull the figures out of PDF invoices and bank statements into Excel. |
| PDF tools and OCR | Convert, merge, split, fill and edit PDFs, and read scanned documents. |
| Clean and standardise | Remove duplicates, fix headers, dates and numbers without writing formulas. |

## Create your account

1. Go to [insight.meldra.ai/register](https://insight.meldra.ai/register).
2. Enter your **Full Name**, **Email**, **Password** and **Confirm Password**.
3. Select **Create Account**.
4. Open the email we send you and verify your address. You cannot sign in until your email is verified.
5. Go to the sign-in page, enter your **Email** and **Password**, and select **Sign In**.
6. If you are asked for a **Verification code**, enter the login code we email to you and select **Verify Code**.

Every new account starts on the Free plan. See [Plans and limits](/help/plans-and-limits) for what each plan includes.

> **Note:** An account can be signed in on two devices at a time. If you sign in on a third, meldra lists your devices. Select **Sign out and continue here** next to the one you want to sign out.

> **Tip:** If your organisation has a meldra licence, sign up with your work email. You may be given a seat automatically. See [Organisations and team licences](/help/organisations-and-team-licences).

## Find your way around

- **Unified Reporting** and **Migration** have their own links in the navigation bar at the top of the page.
- The other tools are grouped in the menus along the same bar.
- Your account menu is at the top right (it shows your email address). It contains **Onboarding**, **Help Center**, **Plan and usage**, **Settings**, **Security** and **Logout**.
