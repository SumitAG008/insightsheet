Excel to PowerPoint turns a workbook into a slide deck. Your tables become PowerPoint tables and your charts are copied across as charts you can still edit. You can also turn a PDF into slides.

## Convert a spreadsheet

1. In the navigation bar, open the **File Conversion** menu and select **Excel to PPT**.
2. Select **Upload Excel or PDF File** and choose your file. You can use `.xlsx`, `.xls`, `.csv` or `.pdf` files.
3. Check the file name and size. To choose another file, select **Remove**.
4. Set the look of the deck under **Presentation design** (see below).
5. Select **Convert to PowerPoint**. A progress bar shows each stage.
6. When you see **Conversion Successful!**, the deck is in your downloads.

Select **Convert Another File** to start again.

The largest file you can convert depends on your plan. The page shows your limit above the upload area. See [Plans and limits](/help/plans-and-limits).

## Choose the design

**Presentation design** appears for spreadsheets only.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Theme | **Light**, **Dark**, **Corporate**, **Warm** or **Green**. Sets the slide background and accent colour. |
| **Brand colour** | Replaces the theme's accent colour. Select **Use theme colour** to go back. |
| **Font** | The font for headings, text and tables, such as Calibri, Arial or Aptos. |
| **Company name (footer)** | Shown at the bottom left of each content slide. |
| **Logo (PNG or JPG, up to 2 MB)** | Shown on cover slides and at the top right of the other slides. PNG, JPG, GIF, BMP and WebP images are accepted. |

The design applies to backgrounds, headings, tables and cover slides. Charts keep their exact Excel look.

meldra remembers your theme, colour, font and company name in this browser for next time. The logo is not remembered: add it again for each conversion.

## What the deck contains

For `.xlsx` and `.csv` files:

- a cover slide with the file name and how many tables, charts and pictures were found;
- a section slide for each tab, when more than one tab has content;
- each table as a PowerPoint table, with up to 15 rows and 10 columns per slide. Longer tables continue on the next slide with the header repeated, and wider tables repeat the first column. Up to 200 rows of each table are included;
- each chart on its own slide, as an editable PowerPoint chart that keeps its Excel formatting;
- each picture on its own slide, at full quality;
- a slide number on each content slide.

A chart or picture that appears on several tabs, such as a logo, is included once.

If your workbook is small enough (3 MB or less), the data behind each chart is embedded, so **Edit Data** works in PowerPoint.

> **Note:** For `.xls` files, and for workbooks meldra cannot read this way, meldra builds the slides from the data instead. The **Presentation design** settings are not applied to those decks.

## Convert a PDF

1. Choose a `.pdf` file.
2. Select **Convert to PowerPoint**.

Each page of the PDF becomes a picture on its own slide. The text on the slides cannot be edited. The page shows how many **Slides Created**.

## Where your data goes

- **Spreadsheets** are sent to meldra's server and converted in memory. They are not stored. No AI is used. Each conversion counts as one conversion and file job. See [Plans and limits](/help/plans-and-limits).
- **PDFs** are converted in your browser. They are not sent to meldra.

> **Note:** On the Free plan, decks made from spreadsheets carry a small meldra.ai mark at the bottom of each slide.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Please select Excel (.xlsx, .xls, .csv) or PDF file" | Choose a file of one of these types. |
| "File size (… MB) exceeds your … MB limit" | Your file is larger than your plan allows. Split it, or see [Plans and limits](/help/plans-and-limits). |
| "Logo must be a PNG, JPG, GIF, BMP or WebP image of 2 MB or less." | Choose a smaller image, or another format. |
| "You have used all … conversions for this month" | Your allowance resets on the 1st, or you can upgrade your plan. |
